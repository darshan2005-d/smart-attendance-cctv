Best Practices for CCTV Recognition Accuracy

  • Diverse Lighting & Angles: Ensure each student's dataset contains straight-on, slight left/right, and slight upward/downward angles matching typical classroom CCTV angles.
  • Resolution: The Hikvision CCTV sub-stream (102) provides low-latency frames (640x360 or 704x576), perfect for real-time 30 FPS processing without frame lag.
  • Clean Face Visibility: Avoid strong backlighting or glare directly behind the student when standing before the camera.
